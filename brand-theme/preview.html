Andrew Dmitriev (aka andmitr) | Personal Brand Color Theme

Dark-first UI with cool steel-blue core (#4A90C2) and warm orange signal accent (#E6522C). Always follow the design token rules: maximum 3 hues per screen, proper contrast ratios, and use accent.base only for the single most important CTA on a screen.
